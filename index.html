From story to scheduled release

Develop the story

Draft horror and mystery narratives, synthesize narration, and create moving visuals with AI-assisted tools.

Edit and review

Assemble picture, narration, sound and captions on separate tracks. Check the finished media and prepare a review copy for the operator.

Prepare the release

Use YouTube Data API v3 to verify the owner’s channel, upload approved videos privately, apply captions and metadata, and schedule publication after review.

Built for one channel

The application is a personal automation tool operated by the owner of What the Night Hides. The owner runs production and controls what is uploaded and when it is released.

This public site provides information and policy documents. It has no sign-in or upload form.
